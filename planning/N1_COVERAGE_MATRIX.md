# JLPT N1 Course Outcomes & Coverage Matrix

- **Level**: `N1` (Advanced Mastery)
- **Prerequisites**: `N5` (Core Kana, Basic Copula/Particles), `N4` (Conditionals/Voice/Keigo), `N3` (Intermediate Grammar), and `N2` (Upper-Intermediate Grammar & Discourse)
- **Structure**: 4 Phases, 16 Original Outcome-Driven Lessons (Lessons 1–16)
- **Languages**: Japanese (Kanji/Kana + Furigana + Romaji), English, Bangla (বাংলা)
- **Content Policy**: 100% original JLPT N1-aligned lessons, dialogues, readings, listening scripts, and practice questions. No textbook or official exam copying.

---

## Phase & Lesson Map

| Phase | Lessons | Focus & Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Editorial, Necessity & Literary Nuance** | Lessons 1–4 | Forced necessity & essence (`を余儀なくされる`, `にほかならない`), enduring emotion & extreme degree (`てやまない`, `極まりない`), synergy & catalysts (`と相まって`, `を皮切りに`), and high-level concession (`ないまでも`, `とはいえ`) |
| **Phase 2: Scope, Authority & Inevitability** | Lessons 5–8 | Unconditional scope (`を問わず`, `いかんによらず`), alignment & context (`に即して`, `をめぐって`), impossibility & inevitability (`べくもない`, `ずにはすまない`), and culmination (`に至っては`, `に至って初めて`) |
| **Phase 3: Hypotheticals, Concession & Extreme Contrast** | Lessons 9–12 | Futility & hypotheticals (`たところで`, `にしたところで`), contingency (`ようによっては`, `次第では`), extreme appearance & wish (`ものなら`, `んばかりに`), and disregard/contrast (`をよそに`, `を尻目に`) |
| **Phase 4: Formal Judgement, Causation & N1 Capstone** | Lessons 13–16 | Unique qualities (`ならではの`, `ただ〜のみ`), threshold shifts (`ともなると`, `ともなれば`), tendencies & compulsion (`きらいがある`, `ずにはおかない`), and formal means/emotion (`をもって`, `に堪えない`) |

## Skill Labs & Practice Assessment Targets

- **Vocabulary (`N1_VOCABULARY`)**: 70 original N1 words (`n1-v001`–`n1-v070`) across `VocabularyCategory` groups, zero ID collision with N5/N4/N3/N2.
- **Kanji (`N1_KANJI`)**: 40 original N1 kanji (zero overlap with N5, N4, N3, and N2 kanji), with readings, stroke counts, radicals, stroke order hints, and compounds.
- **Grammar (`N1_GRAMMAR`)**: 32 grammar patterns (`n1-g01`–`n1-g32`) covering all 16 lessons.
- **Reading (`N1_READINGS`)**: 6 original N1 passages (`n1-read-1`–`n1-read-6`) with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Listening (`N1_LISTENING`)**: 6 original N1 dialogues (`n1-listen-1`–`n1-listen-6`) with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Speaking (`N1_SPEAKING_PROMPTS`)**: 6 original N1 speaking prompts (`n1-speak-1`–`n1-speak-6`).
- **JLPT N1 Practice Set (`N1_EXAM_QUESTIONS`)**: 12 original N1 questions (`n1-exam-1`–`n1-exam-12`, 30-minute timer) across Language Knowledge, Grammar & Reading, and Listening.
