# JLPT N3 Course Outcomes & Coverage Matrix

- **Level**: `N3` (Intermediate)
- **Prerequisites**: `N5` (Core Kana, Basic Copula/Particles, ~103 Kanji, ~156 Vocabulary) and `N4` (Potential/Volitional/Conditionals/Voice/Keigo, 40 Kanji, 70 Vocabulary)
- **Structure**: 4 Phases, 16 Original Outcome-Driven Lessons (Lessons 1–16)
- **Languages**: Japanese (Kanji/Kana + Furigana + Romaji), English, Bangla (বাংলা)
- **Content Policy**: 100% original JLPT N3-aligned lessons, dialogues, readings, listening scripts, and practice questions. No textbook or official exam copying.

---

## Phase & Lesson Map

| Phase | Lessons | Focus & Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Habit Change, Effort & Decisions** | Lessons 1–4 | Gradual change (`ようになる`), conscious effort (`ようにする`), completion/regret (`てしまう`), advance prep (`ておく`), habits/tendencies (`てばかりいる`, `がちだ`), personal vs external decisions (`ことにする`, `ことになる`) |
| **Phase 2: Cause, Credit, Perspective & Contrast** | Lessons 5–8 | Positive credit (`おかげで`) vs blame (`せいで`), standpoint/topic/attitude (`にとって`, `について`, `に対して`), two-sided contrast (`一方で`, `反面`), concession (`にもかかわらず`, `くせに`) |
| **Phase 3: Nuanced Negation, Degree & Means** | Lessons 9–12 | Partial negation (`わけではない`, `とは限らない`), proportional change (`ば〜ほど`), ongoing trends (`ばかりだ`), time windows (`うちに`, `間に`), means/variation/source (`によって`, `によると`) |
| **Phase 4: Supposition, Requests & N3 Capstone** | Lessons 13–16 | Certainty vs impossibility (`に違いない`, `はずがない`), impressions/tendencies (`らしい`, `っぽい`, `気味`), requests & permission (`てほしい`, `てもらいたい`, `させてほしい`), minimal conditions (`さえ〜ば`) & N3 Capstone |

## Skill Labs & Practice Assessment Targets

- **Vocabulary (`N3_VOCABULARY`)**: 70 original N3 words (`n3-v001`–`n3-v070`) across all `VocabularyCategory` groups, zero ID collision with N5/N4.
- **Kanji (`N3_KANJI`)**: 40 original N3 kanji (zero overlap with N5's 103 kanji and N4's 40 kanji), with readings, stroke counts, radicals, stroke order hints, and compounds.
- **Grammar (`N3_GRAMMAR`)**: 32 grammar patterns (`n3-g01`–`n3-g32`) covering all 16 lessons.
- **Reading (`N3_READINGS`)**: 6 original N3 passages (`n3-read-1`–`n3-read-6`) with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Listening (`N3_LISTENING`)**: 6 original N3 dialogues (`n3-listen-1`–`n3-listen-6`) with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Speaking (`N3_SPEAKING_PROMPTS`)**: 6 original N3 speaking prompts (`n3-speak-1`–`n3-speak-6`).
- **JLPT N3 Practice Set (`N3_EXAM_QUESTIONS`)**: 12 original N3 questions (`n3-exam-1`–`n3-exam-12`, 22-minute timer) across Language Knowledge, Grammar & Reading, and Listening.
