# JLPT N4 Course Outcomes & Coverage Matrix

- **Level**: `N4` (Upper-Beginner / Pre-Intermediate)
- **Prerequisites**: `N5` (Core Kana, Basic Copula/Verbs, Particles, ~100 Kanji, ~150+ Core Vocabulary)
- **Structure**: 4 Phases, 16 Original Outcome-Driven Lessons (Lessons 1–16)
- **Languages**: Japanese (Kanji/Kana + Furigana + Romaji), English, Bangla (বাংলা)
- **Content Policy**: 100% original JLPT N4-aligned lessons, dialogues, readings, listening scripts, and practice questions. No textbook or official exam copying.

---

## Phase & Lesson Map

| Phase | Lessons | Focus & Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Capabilities, Intentions & Experiences** | Lessons 1–4 | Potential form (`～られる/～える`), `～てみる` (trying), Volitional form (`～よう` / `～ようと思う`), `～つもりだ` (intentions), `～ておく` (preparation) |
| **Phase 2: Conditionals, Obligations & Giving/Receiving** | Lessons 5–8 | Conditionals (`～ば`, `～なら`, `～と`, `～たら`), Giving/receiving actions (`～てあげる・～てくれる・～てもらう`), `～てしまう` (completion/regret), Obligation & permission nuances |
| **Phase 3: Voice, Transitivity & Indirect Speech** | Lessons 9–12 | Transitive/Intransitive pairs (`自動詞・他動詞`), Passive voice (`～られる`), Causative (`～させる`), Causative-Passive (`～させられる`), Hearsay & appearance (`～そうだ`, `～ようだ`, `～らしい`) |
| **Phase 4: Keigo, Formal Nuances & N4 Capstone** | Lessons 13–16 | Honorifics (`尊敬語`), Humble expressions (`謙譲語`), Purpose & change (`～ようにする`, `～ようになる`), Nominalization & N4 Capstone review |

## Skill Labs & Practice Assessment Targets

- **Vocabulary (`N4_VOCABULARY`)**: 100 original N4 words (`n4-v001`–`n4-v100`) across all `VocabularyCategory` groups.
- **Kanji (`N4_KANJI`)**: 80 original N4 kanji (zero overlap with N5's 103 kanji), with readings, stroke counts, radicals, stroke order hints, and compounds.
- **Grammar (`N4_GRAMMAR`)**: 32 grammar patterns (`n4-g01`–`n4-g32`) covering all 16 lessons.
- **Reading (`N4_READINGS`)**: 6 original N4 passages with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Listening (`N4_LISTENING`)**: 6 original N4 dialogues with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Speaking (`N4_SPEAKING_PROMPTS`)**: 6 original N4 speaking prompts.
- **JLPT N4 Practice Set (`N4_EXAM_QUESTIONS`)**: 12 original N4 questions (20-minute timer) across Language Knowledge, Grammar & Reading, and Listening.
