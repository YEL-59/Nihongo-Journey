# JLPT N2 Course Outcomes & Coverage Matrix

- **Level**: `N2` (Upper-Intermediate)
- **Prerequisites**: `N5` (Core Kana, Basic Copula/Particles, Kanji & Vocabulary), `N4` (Potential/Volitional/Conditionals/Voice/Keigo, 40 Kanji, 70 Vocabulary), and `N3` (Intermediate Grammar, 40 Kanji, 70 Vocabulary)
- **Structure**: 4 Phases, 16 Original Outcome-Driven Lessons (Lessons 1–16)
- **Languages**: Japanese (Kanji/Kana + Furigana + Romaji), English, Bangla (বাংলা)
- **Content Policy**: 100% original JLPT N2-aligned lessons, dialogues, readings, listening scripts, and practice questions. No textbook or official exam copying.

---

## Phase & Lesson Map

| Phase | Lessons | Focus & Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: Formal Occasions, Grounds & Contrast** | Lessons 1–4 | Framing milestones (`にあたって`, `に際して`), citing evidence (`に基づいて`, `を踏まえて`), formal contrast (`にもかかわらず`, `一方で`), and inevitability/risk (`ざるを得ない`, `かねない`) |
| **Phase 2: Scope, Timing & Relationship** | Lessons 5–8 | Scope and domain (`において`, `にわたって`), immediate sequence (`次第`, `た途端`), correlation/connection (`に応じて`, `に伴って`), and non-reliance/indifference (`にかかわらず`, `を問わず`) |
| **Phase 3: Reasoning, Emphasis & Evaluation** | Lessons 9–12 | Inevitable results (`からには`, `以上は`), strong negation/scope (`わけにはいかない`, `どころではない`), exclusive focus (`ばかりか`, `のみならず`), and criteria/evaluation (`からすると`, `からして`) |
| **Phase 4: Nuance, Resolution & N2 Capstone** | Lessons 13–16 | Emotional/logical limits (`ものがある`, `ことだ`), formal conclusions (`にほかならない`, `にすぎない`), hypothetical/concessive limits (`たとしても`, `ないことには`), and N2 Capstone (`つつある`, `てならない`) |

## Skill Labs & Practice Assessment Targets

- **Vocabulary (`N2_VOCABULARY`)**: 70 original N2 words (`n2-v001`–`n2-v070`) across `VocabularyCategory` groups, zero ID collision with N5/N4/N3.
- **Kanji (`N2_KANJI`)**: 40 original N2 kanji (zero overlap with N5, N4, and N3 kanji), with readings, stroke counts, radicals, stroke order hints, and compounds.
- **Grammar (`N2_GRAMMAR`)**: 32 grammar patterns (`n2-g01`–`n2-g32`) covering all 16 lessons.
- **Reading (`N2_READINGS`)**: 6 original N2 passages (`n2-read-1`–`n2-read-6`) with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Listening (`N2_LISTENING`)**: 6 original N2 dialogues (`n2-listen-1`–`n2-listen-6`) with 12 comprehension questions.
- **Speaking (`N2_SPEAKING_PROMPTS`)**: 6 original N2 speaking prompts (`n2-speak-1`–`n2-speak-6`).
- **JLPT N2 Practice Set (`N2_EXAM_QUESTIONS`)**: 12 original N2 questions (`n2-exam-1`–`n2-exam-12`, 25-minute timer) across Language Knowledge, Grammar & Reading, and Listening.
